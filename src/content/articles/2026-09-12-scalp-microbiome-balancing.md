## The Biological Truth: The Microbiome Hierarchy

The scalp is not just skin; it is a complex battlefield of microorganisms. The goal is not to "sterilize" the scalp, but to maintain a precise equilibrium.

**1. Prebiotics (The Fuel):** These are non-digestible fibers (like Inulin) that selectively feed the "guardian" bacteria, such as *Staphylococcus epidermidis*, while starving pathogenic fungi.
**2. Probiotics (The Introducers):** These are live cultures or lysates that introduce beneficial strains to the ecosystem to crowd out opportunistic pathogens.
**3. Postbiotics (The Metabolic Truth):** These are the bio-active byproducts (peptides, organic acids) produced by bacteria. Because live probiotics are unstable in water-based shampoos, **postbiotics (lysates and ferments)** are the gold standard for stability and efficacy.

The ultimate goal is to suppress *Malassezia* (the fungus that triggers dandruff and inflammation) and support *S. epidermidis* (the protector of the follicle).
