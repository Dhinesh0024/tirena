Open pores and acne marks are two of the most searched skin concerns, and also two of the most misunderstood — most products marketed to "shrink pores" can't actually change pore size at all. What they can do is improve how pores and marks *look*, which is a real and worthwhile goal, just a different one than the marketing usually implies.

### What's actually happening with open pores

Pores appear enlarged mainly from excess sebum stretching their walls, plus a loss of collagen and elastin around them as skin ages, which removes the structural support that once kept them looking tighter. No skincare product physically shrinks a pore's actual size — what niacinamide and similar ingredients do is regulate the sebum that stretches pores in the first place, and support the collagen that keeps surrounding skin firm, which together improve the appearance over time.

### Why this pairs well as an AM/PM routine

- **Morning: niacinamide + zinc** — niacinamide is well-evidenced for regulating sebum production and strengthening the skin barrier, while zinc adds redness control and further sebum regulation. This is a "maintenance" step: less oil, calmer skin, and gradual textural improvement through the day.
- **Night: retinol** — supports cell turnover, which helps fade the look of old acne marks over time and improves overall texture, complementing what niacinamide does during the day rather than duplicating it.

Using both isn't overkill — they work through different mechanisms and different times of day, which is exactly the kind of pairing that avoids the over-application problems we covered in our [skin cycling guide](/articles/skin-cycling-the-simple-method-replacing-daily-overload/).

### An honest note on what to expect

Niacinamide is not a treatment for active acne itself — if you're dealing with ongoing breakouts rather than just marks and texture, that's worth a dermatologist conversation rather than relying on serums alone. For marks and pore appearance specifically, expect gradual improvement over 8-12 weeks, not an overnight change.
