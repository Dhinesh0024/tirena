### The Biological Reality: Why You Feel "Heavy"

To fix the problem, we have to stop talking about "toxins" (a word used by people selling tea) and start talking about **fluid dynamics**.

Your body has two primary plumbing systems. The first is the circulatory system (blood). The second is the lymphatic system. While your heart pumps blood, your lymph system has no central pump; it relies on muscle movement and pressure gradients to move fluid.

When this system fails, two things happen:

1.  **Interstitial Edema**: This is a fancy way of saying your blood vessels are "leaking." Fluid escapes the capillaries and pools in the spaces between your cells.
2.  **Lymphatic Stasis**: The "drain" is clogged. The lymphatic system fails to sweep that pooled fluid back toward the heart.

The result is a limb that feels like it's made of wet concrete, regardless of how many calories you've cut this week.
