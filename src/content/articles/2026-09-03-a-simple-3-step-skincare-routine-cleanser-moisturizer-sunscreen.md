Skincare doesn't need to be complicated to work. Dermatologists have long pointed to a simple three-step routine — cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen — as the foundation that does most of the actual work, with everything else (serums, treatments, exfoliants) being additions for specific concerns, not requirements.

### Why just these three

- **Cleanser** removes dirt, oil, and product buildup without stripping your skin's natural barrier — the goal is clean skin, not squeaky-tight skin
- **Moisturizer** supports that same barrier by replacing water the cleanser removes and helping skin retain it
- **Sunscreen** is the one step with the strongest evidence behind it long-term — daily broad-spectrum SPF is one of the few skincare habits directly linked to preventing visible skin aging and reducing skin cancer risk

If you only do three things consistently, these are the three worth doing consistently.

### What to look for in each

**Cleanser:** fragrance-free, and ideally free of harsh sulfates if your skin is dry or sensitive — look for supporting ingredients like niacinamide or glycerin rather than a cleanser that leaves skin feeling tight afterward.

**Moisturizer:** hyaluronic acid or glycerin for hydration, and a texture you'll actually want to reapply daily — a moisturizer that feels heavy or sticky is one you'll eventually stop using.

**Sunscreen:** broad-spectrum (protects against both UVA and UVB), SPF 30 or higher, and a texture suited to your skin type — oily or combination skin does better with a gel or lightweight formula rather than a thick cream-based one.
