Seeing more hair in the shower can be worrying. One useful first step is to distinguish **breakage**—hair snapping along the strand—from **shedding**, when hairs fall from the scalp. They can look similar, and you can experience both. A dermatologist can help identify what’s happening and investigate the cause. [American Academy of Dermatology: Hair loss or shedding?](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/hair-loss/insider/shedding)

A gentle routine can help limit avoidable damage to fragile lengths. It can’t diagnose or treat every cause of shedding or thinning. The American Academy of Dermatology recommends gentle shampooing and using conditioner after washing to help reduce breakage and split ends. [AAD: Hair-loss self-care tips](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/hair-loss/treatment/tips)

### A simple routine

**1. Cleanse your scalp gently.**
Use shampoo on wet hair and massage the scalp with your fingertips rather than scrubbing or tangling the lengths. Wash as often as your scalp needs; there isn’t one schedule that suits everyone. L’Oréal’s current product page gives similar directions for its Fall Resist 3X shampoo: apply to wet hair, massage gently, and rinse thoroughly. [L’Oréal Paris Fall Resist 3X Shampoo](https://www.lorealparis.co.in/fall-resist/3x-shampoo-704-ml)

**2. Condition after shampooing.**
Apply conditioner mainly to the lengths and ends, leave it on for the time stated on the label, then rinse. L’Oréal lists 1–3 minutes for its Fall Resist conditioner. Conditioning helps strands feel smoother and easier to detangle, which may reduce breakage during handling; it doesn’t stop shedding from the scalp. [L’Oréal Paris Fall Resist 3X Conditioner](https://www.lorealparis.co.in/fall-resist/3x-conditioner-192-5-ml)

**3. Handle wet hair with care.**
Hair can be more vulnerable to damage when wet. Blot or wrap it instead of rubbing vigorously with a towel, and detangle gently. Reduce tight styles and frequent high heat if they seem to worsen breakage. [AAD: Hair styling without damage](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/hair-loss/hair-care/styling)

**4. Treat a scalp serum as optional.**
If you choose to try one, follow that product’s directions and stop if it irritates your scalp. Don’t assume a cosmetic serum will regrow hair or address the reason for persistent shedding. If you’re noticing ongoing thinning, a widening part, or sudden or patchy hair loss, get medical advice rather than relying on a product routine. [AAD: Hair-loss tips](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/hair-loss/treatment/tips)
