Dry, frizzy hair can feel rough, tangle easily, or puff up in humid weather. A rinse-off hair mask may help by conditioning the hair surface: conditioner ingredients reduce friction between strands, improve combability, and can make hair feel smoother. These effects improve the hair’s feel and manageability, but they don’t permanently restore damaged hair or erase split ends. [Review of hair-conditioning science](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4387693/) · [Review of hair-care physicochemistry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9921463/)

### What to expect from a hair mask

Think of a mask as a more intensive conditioning step, not a permanent repair treatment. The result depends on the formula and your hair: a rich mask may suit coarse, curly, or very dry lengths, while fine hair may feel weighed down by too much product.

Masks also don’t replace everyday conditioner for everyone. If your hair still feels dry or tangles between wash days, a regular conditioner or a suitable leave-in product may be more useful for your routine. The American Academy of Dermatology advises applying conditioner where your hair needs it; for fine or straight hair, that may be mainly the ends. [AAD: Tips for healthy hair](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/hair-scalp-care/hair/healthy-hair-tips)

### How to use one without weighing hair down

1. **Follow the product label.** Masks differ: some are meant for a few minutes, others for longer. Leaving one on longer than directed isn’t automatically better.
2. **Apply mainly to the lengths and ends.** Keep it off the scalp if your roots get oily or the product’s directions recommend that.
3. **Use an amount that suits your hair.** Start modestly, especially if your hair is fine or easily weighed down.
4. **Rinse thoroughly.** If your hair feels coated or limp afterward, use less next time or use the mask less often.
5. **Reduce friction between washes.** Be gentle when detangling and limit harsh towel rubbing and excessive heat styling, which can contribute to rough, frizzy-looking hair. [AAD: Hair styling without damage](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/hair-loss/hair-care/styling)
