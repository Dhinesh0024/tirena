## The Science: Hardware vs. Software

To understand the shift, you must understand the distinction between **Genetics** and **Epigenetics**.

**Genetics (The Hardware):** These are your SNPs (Single Nucleotide Polymorphisms). They are the inherited instructions that dictate your predisposition to collagen breakdown, sensitivity, or hyperpigmentation. If you have a genetic predisposition to early fine lines, no amount of "moisturizing" will change the blueprint.

**Epigenetics (The Software):** This is the layer of chemical markers (like DNA methylation) that sit on top of your DNA. Epigenetics is where the battle is won. It is how your skin responds to UV radiation, pollution, and cortisol. By targeting the epigenetic markers, we can effectively "silence" the genes responsible for aging and "activate" those responsible for repair.
