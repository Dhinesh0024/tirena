Body wash used to be a commodity aisle — soap-based, interchangeable, chosen mostly by scent. That's changed. The same active-ingredient approach that transformed face skincare over the last decade has moved to body care, and picking a body wash by scent alone now means missing out on formulas that can actually address specific concerns.

### What to look for based on your concern

- **Oily skin or body acne** — look for salicylic acid (BHA), which is oil-soluble and can penetrate into pores to clear the buildup that causes body breakouts, particularly on the back, chest, and shoulders
- **Tan, dark spots, or uneven tone** — glycolic acid and alpha arbutin work well together here: glycolic acid exfoliates surface buildup while alpha arbutin targets pigmentation directly
- **Sensitive or easily irritated skin** — a gentle, glycerin-based formula without added actives is often the better choice; more isn't always better, especially on skin that reacts easily
- **pH matters more than most people realize** — body skin does best with a wash in the 4.5–5.5 pH range; harsher, high-pH soap-based washes can disrupt the skin barrier over time even if they feel "extra clean" in the shower
