An oily scalp usually comes down to overactive sebaceous glands, product buildup, or washing too infrequently (or, sometimes, too often with the wrong shampoo). Before reaching for a product, it helps to understand which of these is driving it for you.

## What to look for in a shampoo

- **Clarifying agents** (like salicylic acid) to break down buildup without stripping hair dry
- **Lightweight, water-based formulas** rather than heavy conditioning shampoos
- **Balanced pH** to avoid triggering more oil production as a rebound effect

## What to avoid

Avoid shampoos marketed as "ultra-moisturizing" if oil — not dryness — is the issue. These can leave hair looking greasy within a day.
