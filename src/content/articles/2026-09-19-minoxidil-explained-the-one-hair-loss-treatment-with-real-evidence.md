Most hair growth products marketed online lean heavily on new-sounding ingredients and thin evidence — we covered exactly this problem in our [peptide scalp serum breakdown](/articles/peptide-scalp-serums-the-skinification-of-hair-explained/). Minoxidil is the opposite case: it's been the American Academy of Dermatology's first-line recommendation for pattern hair loss for decades, and it's the only topical treatment with FDA approval specifically for hair regrowth without a prescription in most markets.

### How it actually works

Minoxidil was originally developed as a blood pressure medication — researchers noticed unexpected hair growth as a side effect, which led to its topical use for hair loss. It works as a vasodilator, widening blood vessels to increase blood flow to hair follicles, and extends the anagen (active growth) phase of the hair cycle. Even after decades of use, researchers don't fully understand every mechanism — but the clinical results are well documented regardless.

### Foam vs. liquid

- **Liquid solution** — contains propylene glycol, which helps minoxidil absorb but is also the main cause of scalp irritation and itching for many users
- **Foam** — generally causes less irritation and dries faster, but can be harder to apply evenly through long or thick hair
- Both are equally effective when the concentration matches — the choice comes down to your scalp's sensitivity and your hair length, not effectiveness

### Concentration matters

5% is the standard recommendation for men, showing roughly 45% more regrowth than 2% after 48 weeks in clinical data. Women were originally only approved for 2%, though 5% foam has since been approved for women too — higher concentrations were historically avoided due to a small risk of unwanted facial hair growth.

### What to actually expect

- **First 2-4 weeks:** increased shedding is common and expected — this is a sign the hair cycle is being disrupted and reset, not a sign it's failing
- **4-6 months:** the realistic timeline before you can judge whether it's working
- **Ongoing use required:** minoxidil maintains results only while you continue using it — stopping typically reverses the gains over several months

### Before you start

Because minoxidil is an active drug with real physiological effects (including on blood pressure historically), and because purchasing it in India often goes through a pharmacy rather than casual retail, **talk to a dermatologist before starting** — especially to confirm it's the right treatment for your specific type of hair loss, since minoxidil works best for hereditary thinning at the crown and is less effective for a receding hairline.
